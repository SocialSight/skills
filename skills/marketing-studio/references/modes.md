# Modes

| Mode | Their words | Cast | Shots, camera, and motion | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ugc` | real person, TikTok, organic, testimonial | one presenter talking to camera | handheld phone, arm's length, natural light, quick cuts | casual speech |
| `tutorial` | how to use, steps, show how | hands, presenter optional | overhead and close-ups, one step per shot, in order | VO explains each step |
| `unboxing` | unboxing, just arrived, package | hands, then a face | top-down box → open → reveal → first reaction | tape, paper, a gasp |
| `review` | review, honest opinion, worth it | presenter seated facing camera | medium talking shot + cutaways to details, verdict last | presenter speaks |
| `showcase` | product video, clean, no people, catalog | none | studio or styled surface, slow orbit, macro details, light sweep | music only |
| `tv_spot` | commercial, TV ad, cinematic, premium | actors in a scene | hook → problem → product → payoff → end card; dolly, crane, graded light | music + optional VO |
| `try_on` | wear, try on, outfit, lookbook | model wearing or using it | mirror, turn, full length + detail; organic or polished per their words | music or light talk |
| `conceptual` | surreal, levitating, splash, CGI, creative | none | floating product, splash, sculptural set, slow motion | music, sound design |
| `explainer` | explain, show how it works, editorial, motion comic, animated story, illustrated, documentary | an illustrated or animated lead, often one figure; the idea is the hero | 2D / illustrated frames, slow push-ins, parallax between layers, one dramatic transition, extreme close-up payoff | narrator + subtitles or labels, restrained music under the voice |
| `app_demo` | app, website, marketplace, SaaS, "found it on", screenshots, product discovery | one presenter to camera; the app's screens as overlays | hook → discovery → UI demo → social proof → payoff → CTA; direct-to-camera, jump cuts, digital punch-ins, screenshots sliding over the presenter | presenter speaks fast, bold keywords in sync, UI clicks and whooshes |

The Sound column is the mode's default. A brief's sound (narration /
dialogue / music_only) and text (subtitles / keywords / labels / none)
override it: a `tv_spot` with a narrator, a `ugc` where nobody talks, an
`explainer` with music only.

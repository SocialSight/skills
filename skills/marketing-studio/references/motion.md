# Motion

Write every video beat as camera · action · **motion** · sound. Motion is
one camera move plus what moves in frame:

slow push-in, pull-back reveal, orbit, dolly left / right, tracking shot,
whip pan, handheld drift, crane up, macro rack focus, slow motion.

Example: "0–3s: medium shot, she lifts the bag to the window light; slow
push-in, the strap swings; café murmur."

- One move per beat. Two moves in one beat blur.
- Stills are frames: no motion words in an image prompt.
- When stills or a start image anchor the video, describe the motion between
  them, not what the frames already show.
- Phrase positively ("tack sharp", "empty street"), not "no blur".
